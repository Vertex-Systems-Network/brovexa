# Brovexa — Packages, Research Credits & Billing Economics ADR v1.0

Status: **Planning Only — commercial mechanics baseline; final prices/provider require validation**

## Pricing model

**Subscription + included Research Credits + optional controlled top-ups.**

Ordinary navigation, viewing existing intelligence, saved views, notes/tasks and routine CRM work do not burn credits. Credits cover cost-bearing external research/enrichment/AI/monitoring work.

## Accounting layers

Keep separate:

1. **Provider Cost Ledger** — actual/estimated monetary COGS.
2. **Usage Meter** — operations such as search/fetch/enrichment/model tokens/embedding/storage.
3. **Research Credit Ledger** — customer-facing commercial units generated by versioned `CreditRateTable` mappings.

Provider-price changes must not rewrite historical customer credit ledgers.

## Credit ledger

Canonical records include CreditAccount/Bucket/Grant/Reservation/Usage/Release/Adjustment/Expiry/Refund, CreditRateTable version, UsageEvent and ProviderCostEvent. Mutations are immutable/idempotent/auditable.

Separate included, top-up, promotional/trial and compensation buckets.

## Customer-friendly charging

- preflight estimates credits before meaningful ResearchJobs
- reserve bounded amount where useful
- settle only completed eligible billable operations
- retries/replays never double-charge
- internal retry/bug overhead is not separately charged
- policy-blocked/unsupported stages consume no credits
- cancellation settles only completed disclosed billable operations
- estimate growth beyond limits pauses/reviews
- uncapped surprise overage is **off by default**
- auto top-up/overage requires explicit opt-in/cap/notification

Included monthly credits normally reset per billing period; paid/top-up and promotional expiry is a separately disclosed policy requiring legal/commercial validation.

## Billable classes

Potential credits: paid discovery/search/local APIs, controlled web research/fetches, licensed data/enrichment/verification, deep AI extraction/reasoning, embeddings within billable research and recurring monitoring that incurs provider/model cost.

Routine low-cost UI summarization should normally be bundled rather than making the product feel punitive.

## Preflight

Show ranges: candidate records, source coverage, requests/fetches/enrichments, AI stages, credit range, user hard cap, runtime/coverage uncertainty and restricted/skipped sources.

Do not present fake precision.

## Package hypothesis

### Free — $0
1 seat, small monthly credits, small jobs, low concurrency, Business 360/website status, limited signal/opportunity preview, basic extension capture; no/limited bulk export/API/scheduling.

### Launch — proposed $49/mo
1 seat, larger credits, full core discovery/research/intelligence/Lead OS, Desktop/extensions, saved jobs, standard exports, AI Lead Copilot, bounded monitoring.

### Pro — proposed $149/mo
3 seats, pooled credits, higher concurrency, scheduled monitoring, advanced signals/scoring, roles/assignment, starter CRM/webhooks, richer exports/history.

### Growth — proposed $399/mo
10 seats, larger pooled credits/concurrency, continuous monitoring, API/webhooks, supported CRM sync, advanced routing/scoring, Market Intelligence, advanced compliance/audit.

### Enterprise — custom
Negotiated seats/credits/concurrency, SSO/SCIM where supported, custom RBAC/retention, approved private connectors, SLA/support/onboarding/invoicing/custom terms.

Prices remain provisional.

## Machine-readable entitlements

PlanVersion contains explicit keys such as seats, monthly credits, max job credits/concurrency, schedules/monitors, export rows, API/rate limits, CRM sync, webhooks, Desktop/extensions, advanced routing, Agent Center/custom signals, audit retention and SSO.

UI does not hardcode package name to behavior.

## Versioning

`Plan` and immutable `PlanVersion` are separate. Public pricing/limit changes create new versions. Existing customer migration/grandfathering is explicit.

## Upgrade/downgrade

Upgrade preview shows recurring price/proration/credit/seat changes and provisions only after server-verified billing state.

Downgrade never silently deletes data. Excess seats/jobs/monitors/data move to explicit limited/read-only/paused states based on policy; customer resolves blockers before effective downgrade where needed.

Cancellation defaults to period-end where commercially appropriate. Payment failure/cancellation does not silently delete data; grace/read-only/archive/delete states are explicit.

## Payment provider candidates

### Paddle — preferred launch validation candidate
Current public pay-as-you-go pricing is 5% + $0.50 per Checkout transaction. Paddle acts as Merchant of Record and documents global tax/compliance/billing/fraud handling and selling in 200+ countries/territories.

Validate operating-entity approval, payouts/currencies, B2B invoicing/bank transfers, checkout/payment methods, API/webhook/sandbox, top-up model and effective ARPU economics.

### Lemon Squeezy — MoR fallback
Current public pricing is 5% + $0.50; supports subscriptions, portal and usage-based billing. Validate enterprise invoicing, eligibility and long-term B2B API needs.

### Stripe — direct control candidate
Strong Checkout/Billing/portal/tax capabilities; economics depend on entity/payment mix/region and should include Billing/Tax/fraud/accounting/tax-registration overhead, not just headline card fees.

Use if control/economics/enterprise needs materially outweigh MoR simplicity and operating entity is eligible.

## Provider abstraction

Brovexa owns BillingCustomer, Subscription, PlanVersion, Entitlement, CheckoutAttempt, Invoice/Payment/Refund references, BillingEvent and ProviderMapping.

Webhooks are external events: verify signatures, IDs/idempotency, account mappings and state transitions. Browser redirect success never grants entitlements alone. Periodic reconciliation catches missing/out-of-order events/provider outages.

## Unit economics

`Net Revenue = Price - payment/MoR fees - merchant costs not handled - refund/support allowance`

`Variable COGS = source APIs + enrichment + AI + fetch/compute + evidence storage + variable infrastructure`

`Gross Margin = (Net Revenue - Variable COGS) / Net Revenue`

Model median, P75, P90 heavy users, maximum allowed usage, failure/retry overhead, free abuse, annual discount and top-up margin.

Exact credits are locked only after representative Quick/Standard/Deep pilot jobs measure real source/model cost distributions.
